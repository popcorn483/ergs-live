ERGS Live privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

ERGS Live shows live scores, fixtures and results for Enniskillen Royal Grammar School (ERGS) rugby. It is run by the school’s rugby coaches. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

If you just follow the scores

You don’t need an account. The app does not collect your name, email, location or contacts, and it has no adverts, analytics or tracking. It remembers which score updates your device has already shown, so the animations don’t repeat. That small note stays on your device.

Coaches and players who sign in

Coaches and squad players can sign in with their Google account. We use the email address only to check whether it is on the school’s list of coaches or players, so we can unlock the right features (scoring for coaches; match videos, photos and records for coaches and players). Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see your Google password.

What is shown publicly

  • Fixtures, results, scores and the match timeline, which can include the names of pupils who scored.
  • Small headshots of scorers in the score animations, where the coaches have added a player photo.
  • Postponement and cancellation notices.

Match video and photo links, the record against each school, leaderboards, Team of the Week nominations and coaches’ notes are only visible to signed-in coaches and players (notes to coaches only).

Where the information is kept

Scores and team information are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), run by Google on behalf of the school’s rugby department. Information is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Your choices

If you would like a pupil’s name or photo removed from the app, or have any question about this policy, contact the ERGS rugby coaches at ergsrugby@gmail.com (address shown here as text too: ergsrugby@gmail.com). Removal requests are handled promptly.
